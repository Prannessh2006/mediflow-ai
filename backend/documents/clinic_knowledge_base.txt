EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
====================
For life-threatening emergencies:
1. Call 112 (National Emergency Number)
2. Call 108 (Ambulance Service)
3. MediFlow Emergency Line: +91-9876543210

Symptoms requiring immediate emergency care:
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Sudden severe headache
- Signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
- Severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis)
- Seizures
- Suspected poisoning or overdose

PRE-PROCEDURE PREPARATION
==========================
Fasting Blood Tests:
- Fast for 8-12 hours before the test
- Water intake is allowed
- Continue regular medications unless advised otherwise

Blood Pressure Monitoring:
- Avoid caffeine 30 minutes before
- Rest quietly for 5 minutes before measurement
- Avoid exercise before the test

ECG (Electrocardiogram):
- No special preparation required
- Inform doctor of all medications
- Wear comfortable, loose clothing
